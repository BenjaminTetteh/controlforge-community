# ControlForge Community

> **Modernizing IT Audit through automation, analytics, and governance.**

ControlForge Community is the open-source edition of **ControlForge**, an Audit Operations Platform designed to help IT Auditors, Internal Audit teams, GRC professionals, and Security teams automate repetitive audit activities, analyze governance data, and produce executive-ready reporting.

Originally created to eliminate spreadsheet-driven User Access Reviews (UARs) and IT General Controls (ITGC) testing, ControlForge has evolved into a platform that streamlines audit workflows from evidence onboarding through findings, remediation, and executive reporting.

---

# 🚀 Why ControlForge?

Traditional IT audits often rely on manual spreadsheets, disconnected evidence, repetitive testing, and time-consuming reporting.

ControlForge helps modernize that process by bringing audit activities into a single platform that supports:

- Evidence onboarding
- Audit analytics
- IT control testing
- Findings management
- Governance dashboards
- Executive reporting
- Remediation tracking

The goal is simple:

> **Spend less time manipulating spreadsheets and more time delivering audit insights.**

# 📖 Project Vision

ControlForge is being developed to help organizations move beyond spreadsheet-driven audits toward secure, automated, and continuously governed audit operations.

Rather than replacing auditor judgment, ControlForge is designed to augment it by reducing manual effort, improving consistency, and enabling auditors to focus on risk analysis and decision-making.
